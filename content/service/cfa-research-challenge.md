I serve as Faculty Advisor for Kean University's team in the [CFA Institute Research Challenge](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/events/research-challenge), an annual global equity research competition. Student teams analyze a publicly traded company, write an equity research report with a buy, sell, or hold recommendation, and present their findings to industry professionals.

### 2026 - 2027 Season

This season is Kean's first in the competition. Our team of four undergraduate students is analyzing Compass, Inc. and competes in the CFA Society New York local round, which this season includes 37 teams from 28 universities. Winners of the local round advance to the sub-regional, regional, and global rounds.
