## Student Competition Advising

Faculty Advisor, [CFA Institute Research Challenge](/service/cfa-research-challenge/). In this annual global equity research competition, student teams analyze a publicly traded company, write a research report with a buy, sell, or hold recommendation, and present it to industry professionals.

**2026 - 2027 Season** (4 students)

- Kean University's first season in the competition. The team is analyzing Compass, Inc. in the CFA Society New York local round.

## Student Research Mentoring

Faculty Mentor, [Kean Research Days](https://www.kean.edu/research-days). Each year I mentor undergraduate teams developing and presenting independent research projects.

**2026** (23 students)

- Seven poster presentations on passive-ownership and mega-cap concentration risk, thematic and AI-managed ETFs, and the valuation of AI.
- One oral presentation, ["The Structural Shift in Firm Innovation via AI Orientation"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5grMaITTC9I).

**2025** (20 students)

- Five poster presentations on a range of ETF topics.

**2024** (27 students)

- Six poster presentations on industry and macroeconomic topics, including the U.S. electric vehicle industry, inflation, and artificial intelligence in healthcare.

**2022** (30 students)

- Nine poster presentations on ETF performance and flows, ESG and cryptocurrency ETFs, and financial markets during the COVID-19 pandemic.
